PILOT REGISTRATION

Why do I need a nickname?
The leaderboard can be seen by everyone, so it shows only your nickname — never your real name. Your real name, class and seat number are sent only to your teacher.
Rules: 2–12 letters, numbers or Chinese characters (no spaces). Rude or offensive words in any language are not allowed, and your teacher can change a nickname at any time.

MISSION SETUP

How to play
  • Type the word on an enemy to lock on and fire. Finish the word to destroy it.
  • Enemies that reach you cost 1 ♥. Lose all ♥ and the mission ends.
  • Backspace releases your lock. Esc pauses.
  • Destroyed enemies may drop items: 1 💣 clear screen, 2 ❄️ freeze 5 s, 3 🛡️ shield.
  • Boss: type its word to damage it. Type the words on its missiles to shoot them down!
  • Many mechs have a special move: type words in a row to charge it, then press Space.
  • Multiplayer: anyone can shoot any enemy — whoever finishes the word first gets the points!
